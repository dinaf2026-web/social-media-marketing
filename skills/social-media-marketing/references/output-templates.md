## 30/60/90-day plan

### Days 1–30 — Foundation + testing
- positioning/profile fixes
- production system
- initial repeatable series
- baseline metrics

### Days 31–60 — Double down
- identify strongest themes/formats
- improve packaging and retention
- test conversion paths
- build proof/community loops

### Days 61–90 — Scale + refine
- scale repeatable winners
- repurpose intelligently
- introduce partnerships/monetization where appropriate
- formal review and next-quarter decisions
